## Experiments

### Pain framing and landing intent
- **Experiment code:** pain_frame_intent
- **Name:** Pain framing and landing intent
- **Hypothesis:** For the provisional audience, posts framing plans that fail on messy weeks will produce a higher landing_cta_clicked rate per attributable landing session than posts framing broken-streak pressure.
- **Variable being tested:** Messy-week planning pain versus broken-streak pressure, holding format, CTA family, publishing window, and destination consistent.
- **Control or baseline:** Broken-streak framing is the comparison baseline; no historical winner is assumed.
- **Target audience:** Problem-aware Instagram users frustrated by rigid habit systems.

#### Content pillars
- real_life_friction
- **Primary metric:** landing_cta_clicked events divided by attributable landing sessions for each message variant

#### Secondary metrics
- Attributable landing sessions by utm_content
- auth_started events by variant if attribution is retained
- **Success criteria:** Select a direction only if each variant has at least 30 attributable landing sessions and one variant's CTA-click rate is at least 25% higher than the other.
- **Failure criteria:** Neither variant reaches 30 attributable landing sessions, tracking cannot retain variant attribution, or the rate difference is below 25%.
- **Minimum evidence needed:** At least two posts per pain frame, matched across one static and one carousel where possible, and 30 attributable landing sessions per variant.
- **Decision enabled:** Choose the dominant pain frame for the next period or repeat the test unchanged if evidence is insufficient.

### Product proof format
- **Experiment code:** proof_format_intent
- **Name:** Product proof format
- **Hypothesis:** A carousel that connects a real-life problem to a verified product mechanism will produce a higher landing_cta_clicked rate per attributable landing session than a static post using the same mechanism and CTA.
- **Variable being tested:** Carousel versus static presentation of the same product mechanism.
- **Control or baseline:** Static product-proof post; no historical format winner is assumed.
- **Target audience:** Solution-curious users who need evidence that adaptive habits are concrete.

#### Content pillars
- adaptive_mechanism
- **Primary metric:** landing_cta_clicked events divided by attributable landing sessions by format

#### Secondary metrics
- Attributable landing sessions by utm_content
- auth_started events by format
- Instagram reach and saves if manually imported from Metricool
- **Success criteria:** Prefer a format only if each cell has at least 30 attributable landing sessions and its CTA-click rate exceeds the other by at least 25%.
- **Failure criteria:** Insufficient sessions, unequal destination or CTA, missing UTM attribution, or less than 25% rate separation.
- **Minimum evidence needed:** At least two matched static/carousel pairs using verified mechanisms and 30 attributable landing sessions per format.
- **Decision enabled:** Allocate more next-period product-proof posts to the more efficient format, or preserve a balanced mix if inconclusive.

### Try versus co-creation invitation
- **Experiment code:** early_adopter_activation
- **Name:** Try versus co-creation invitation
- **Hypothesis:** An explicit early-adopter co-creation invitation will produce a higher auth_started rate per attributable landing session than a generic try-the-product invitation.
- **Variable being tested:** Transparent co-creation/feedback CTA versus generic try CTA.
- **Control or baseline:** Generic try-the-product invitation.
- **Target audience:** Users who recognize rigid-habit pain and accept an early-stage product.
